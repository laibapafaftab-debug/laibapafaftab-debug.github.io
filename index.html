How it runs

Four stages, always in this order.

So you always know what's happening and when — no surprises along the way.

STEP 01

Discovery call

We talk through your business, your customers, and what the site needs to do. 20–30 minutes, over call or WhatsApp.

STEP 02

Design draft

You get a visual layout of the homepage before any code is written, so there are no surprises later.

STEP 03

Build & review

The full site gets built and tested on mobile, tablet, and desktop. You review and request changes.

STEP 04

Launch & support

Site goes live on your domain. You get a short walkthrough plus support for edits afterward.
